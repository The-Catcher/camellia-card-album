# Camellia Sonata Creative Brief

## Project
Camellia Sonata / 山茶奏鸣

## Product
Black-and-white cultural/creative collectible card album.

## Audience
People who like refined stationery, music-themed merchandise, botanical illustration, and soft literary collectibles.

## Visual Tone
Classical romantic plus fresh literary. The design should feel gentle, elegant, collectible, and breathable.

## Core Motifs
- Camellia flowers as the emotional and visual identity.
- Piano details as rhythm, structure, and musical atmosphere.

## Main Direction
Use white or ivory backgrounds, fine-line camellia drawings, pale gray shading, and cropped piano details such as keys, strings, hammers, music stand edges, or soft shadows.

## Constraints
- No color accents.
- No heavy explanatory text.
- No character-card system.
- No dark gothic treatment.
- Avoid full piano illustrations except on a special card if needed.

## Required Deliverables
- One cover concept.
- Six card artworks: Prelude, Adagio, Nocturne, Waltz, Echo, Finale.
- One contact sheet.
- Production notes.
- Review checklist.
