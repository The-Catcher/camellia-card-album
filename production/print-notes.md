# Camellia Sonata Print Notes

## Size
- Card trim: 70 mm x 100 mm.
- Bleed: 3 mm on all sides.
- Safe area: 5 mm inside trim.

## Paper
Preferred options:
- Matte art paper for a clean gallery-like finish.
- Fine-textured paper for a literary stationery feeling.
- Pearl white card stock for a delicate gift quality.

## Finish
Recommended:
- Spot UV on selected petal highlights or piano-key reflections.
- Light embossing or debossing on black piano-key areas.

Optional:
- Silver-gray foil only if the producer confirms it remains acceptable within the black-and-white palette.

## Export
- Print PDF with bleed.
- Preview PNG or JPG for digital review.
- Keep all text converted to outlines or include font files according to printer requirements.

## Inspection
- Confirm no colored accents were introduced.
- Confirm fine linework remains printable.
- Confirm text stays inside safe area.
- Confirm dark areas do not lose piano-key detail.

## Current Source Files
This workspace contains editable SVG concept files rather than production PDF exports. Open the SVG files in Illustrator, Figma, Affinity Designer, Inkscape, or another vector editor to refine artwork and export final print PDFs.
