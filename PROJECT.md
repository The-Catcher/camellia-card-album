# Camellia Sonata / 山茶奏鸣

Black-and-white cultural/creative collectible card album themed around camellia flowers and piano details.

## Direction

- Style: classical romantic plus fresh literary
- Product: pure visual collectible card album
- Palette: white, ivory, gray, charcoal, and black
- Motifs: fine-line camellia illustration with cropped piano details

## Figma

Editable Figma design file: https://www.figma.com/design/85QMN12UzNZLp8BwoxkQsj

## Source Structure

- `creative-brief/` - project brief and design direction
- `docs/superpowers/specs/` - approved design spec
- `docs/superpowers/plans/` - implementation plan
- `visual-system/` - editable styleboard source
- `layout/` - cover, card concepts, and contact sheet SVG files
- `production/` - print notes and review checklist

## Production Note

The current artwork is delivered as editable SVG concepts and editable Figma frames. Import or refine the SVG files in Figma, Illustrator, Affinity Designer, or Inkscape for final print PDF export.
