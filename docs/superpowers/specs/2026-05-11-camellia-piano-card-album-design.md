# Camellia Piano Card Album Design

## Overview
Design a monochrome cultural/creative collectible card album themed around camellia flowers and piano details. The visual direction combines classical romance with a fresh literary tone: elegant, soft, and collectible, without becoming heavy or overly theatrical.

Working title: **Camellia Sonata / 山茶奏鸣**

## Goals
- Create a black-and-white card album system for pure visual collectible cards.
- Combine camellia and piano imagery in a refined, indirect way.
- Keep the design light, romantic, and suitable for cultural/creative merchandise.
- Use minimal text, limited to titles, numbering, and discreet series marks.

## Visual Direction
The primary direction uses a white or ivory-white base with fine-line camellia illustration. Piano imagery appears as partial details rather than full instruments: piano keys, strings, hammers, music stand edges, or soft shadows.

The cards should feel like a meeting point between a music recital program, a botanical drawing, and a personal collection card. The camellia carries the emotional softness; the piano details carry rhythm, structure, and classical atmosphere.

## Palette
- Base: white, ivory white, or very pale warm gray.
- Linework: charcoal black, soft black, and mid gray.
- Shadow: pale gray washes or stippled graphite texture.
- Accent: no color accents. Contrast and material finish create hierarchy.

## Typography
Use a refined serif typeface for titles and a clean sans serif or small caps style for numbering. Typography should be quiet and secondary to the artwork.

Suggested text hierarchy:
- Album title: thin serif, generous spacing.
- Card title: small serif or small caps.
- Card number: very small, positioned like a collector's edition mark.

## Cover Design
The cover should use large areas of negative space. A camellia branch or single bloom can extend from one corner, overlapping a cropped piano-key detail. The title sits calmly in the open space, avoiding a centered poster-like treatment.

Recommended cover composition:
- White or ivory base.
- Large fine-line camellia from lower right or upper left.
- Cropped piano keys running diagonally or horizontally as a subtle structural element.
- Small title block: `Camellia Sonata / 山茶奏鸣`.

## Card System
Create a series of 6 to 8 cards. Each card keeps consistent margins, numbering, and text placement. Variation comes from flower posture, piano detail, and composition rhythm.

Suggested card titles:
- Prelude
- Adagio
- Nocturne
- Waltz
- Echo
- Finale

Each card should work as a standalone visual collectible while still clearly belonging to the same series.

## Composition Rules
- Keep text minimal and small.
- Avoid full piano illustrations unless used on a special card.
- Use piano details as structure, shadow, or rhythm.
- Let camellia linework remain the main visual identity.
- Preserve generous margins so the set feels refined and breathable.
- Use gray layers to add depth without breaking the black-and-white constraint.

## Material And Finish
Recommended base materials:
- Matte art paper for clean gallery-like presentation.
- Fine-textured paper for a literary handmade feeling.
- Pearl white card stock if a more delicate gift quality is desired.

Recommended finish:
- Spot UV on selected petal highlights or piano-key reflections.
- Light embossing or debossing on black piano-key areas.
- Optional silver-gray foil only if strict black-and-white production allows metallic gray.

## Out Of Scope
- Heavy text descriptions or educational notes.
- Color floral accents.
- Character cards or story-driven cards.
- Dark gothic visual treatment.
- Full marketing campaign design.

## Open Production Decisions
- Exact card count: 6 or 8.
- Final card dimensions.
- Whether the title should be bilingual or Chinese-only.
- Whether metallic gray or silver process counts as acceptable within the black-and-white palette.
